# Implant catalog

Source: https://trello.com/b/FPK4H0oH, Implants column, retrieved 2026-09-09.

40 registered items; Cortex Connector is intentionally a non-installable placeholder with the vanilla structure-void icon. Its effect and slot await specification. All other item textures are the original 32×32 Trello attachments.

Each body slot accepts one implant. Bonuses stack only when the card says “per arm” or “per hand”; other duplicated implants activate their effect once. Installed implants survive death and travel, and are saved per UUID in the overworld data file. Removing an implant returns its original item NBT.

Recipes use four iron, two copper, two redstone and the listed core; these are initial balancing choices.
